Before You Say “Oui”

Paris offers unmatched romance, architecture and gastronomy — a city that turns every wedding into a cinematic story, with iconic backdrops found nowhere else in the world.

Just as importantly, Paris is one of the world’s most accessible destination wedding locations. With direct international flights and high-speed rail connections from across Europe, it’s remarkably easy for family and friends to join your celebration. Effortless travel, exceptional hospitality and unforgettable experiences make Paris one of the most desirable wedding destinations in the world.

Yes. Civil marriage in France requires residency conditions, which is why most international couples hold a legal ceremony at home and celebrate their symbolic wedding in Paris — we handle every detail of that celebration.

Every celebration is bespoke, and budgets vary depending on the venue, guest count and overall experience.

A luxury wedding for around 40 guests in one of Paris’ iconic five-star hotels generally starts from €100,000.

If you prefer the charm and privacy of a French château just outside Paris, a comparable celebration typically starts from €55,000, offering a completely different atmosphere and guest experience.
